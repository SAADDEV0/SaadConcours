### Business English test — Bachelor Excellence — Auditing and Management Control

**Task 1 : ACCOUNTING**

> Accounting is concerned with the records of a business. These records are an important source of Information. They give a businessman an idea about the present financial situation, the total income he has earned, and the necessary measures to take in the future. The recording of this information is known as bookkeeping. It is a system in which debit and credit entries are recorded. First, a bookkeeper enters the transactions in a book called a journal. Then he transfers the entries into the ledger. This operation is called posting.
>
> Quarterly or annually, bookkeepers have to present financial statements. The businessman may want to know the difference between what the firm has spent and what it has earned. This financial statement is known as a balance sheet.
>
> Recently most firms are using computers instead of books. Besides saving time and money, computers perform other tasks such as paying bills, controlling inventory and writing cheques.
>
> Financial statements are not only important for planning but also for preparing the firm's income tax return. An auditor may carry out a detailed inspection of the accounts of a firm. However large companies may require an external audit. It is carried out by a certified accountant who is professionally trained to uncover fraud or mistakes.

*Comprehension :*

1. Why is accounting an important source of information?
2. Define Posting.
3. List all the advantages of a balance sheet.
4. What advantages does a computer offer?
5. Why do firms resort to an external audit?
