## Méthode

Quatre mini-cas en 2 heures : comptabilité analytique (8 points), opérations sur effets de commerce (4), impôt sur le revenu salarial (5) et une question d'analyse financière en anglais (4). L'énoncé 1 se vérifie de lui-même : le coût de revient tombe exactement sur le coût maximal compatible avec la marge attendue.

## Énoncé 4 — Which situation should be presented to the bank?

**Situation A.** The three companies show the same net income (150), but not the same *quality* of income:

- In **A**, the net income comes almost entirely from the core business (operating income 160). This profit is recurring and shows that the activity itself generates cash to repay the loan. The small financial loss (−20) is normal for an indebted firm.
- In **B**, the core business loses money (−10); the profit comes from financial income (170), which is volatile and not linked to the company's main activity.
- In **C**, two thirds of the profit come from non-operating income (100), such as the sale of an asset: an exceptional, non-recurring gain.

A banker looks at the company's ability to generate **sustainable operating cash flows** (EBITDA, operating income) to repay the loan. Situation A is therefore the most convincing.
